TANDEM
Watch YouTube and your own videos together with someone far away.

BEFORE YOU START
  Install VLC, the video player:  https://www.videolan.org/vlc/
  Optional: FFmpeg, for pictures of your videos in the library.

START
  Windows:  double-click Tandem.bat
            A black window opens and stays open; that is Tandem running.
            Windows may ask whether to allow it. Choose "Run anyway" / "Allow".
  Linux:    run ./tandem in a terminal
            (also: sudo apt install vlc ffmpeg)

  Your web browser opens with your video library. The first time, Tandem
  looks through your home folder for videos larger than 200 MB. Change the
  folders in Settings (profile picture, top right).

WATCH TOGETHER
  1. Click "Watch Together".
  2. One of you clicks "Create room" and sends the 6-character code to the
     other, who types it in and clicks "Join".
  3. YouTube: paste a link and click "Play for both".
     Your own videos: while in the room, go to your library and press Play
     on a video. You both need the same video file on your computers.
  Play, pause and seek on either side apply to both of you.

  If it says it can't reach the sync server, open Settings and check that
  "Sync server address" is the same on both computers.

QUIT
  Close the black window (Windows), press Ctrl+C in the terminal (Linux),
  or choose "Quit Tandem" from the profile menu in the page.

Nothing is uploaded. Your videos never leave your computer; only play,
pause and seek messages go between you.
